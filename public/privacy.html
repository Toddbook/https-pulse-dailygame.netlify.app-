Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

In short: the PULSE Daily app collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your scores stay on your phone.

Who we are

PULSE Daily is a daily memory game made in the UK by an independent developer. You can contact us at tbookatz@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

The iOS and Android apps

  • Scores, streaks and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Daily reminders are optional. If you turn them on, your phone schedules them itself. No server is involved and we can't see whether you use them.
  • Sharing a result opens your phone's own share sheet. We only see what you choose to post publicly.
  • The app works offline and doesn't contact our servers while you play. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • Apple and Google may collect data about app downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies.

The website

  • Scores and streaks are saved in your browser's local storage on your device only.
  • Visit counter: each page load adds one to a single total. We store nothing about the visitor: no cookies, IP addresses or identifiers.
  • Hosting: the site runs on Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy.
  • Mailing list: if you sign up for launch news, your email address is collected through Tally and used only to tell you about PULSE. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • PULSE+ on the website: if you subscribe, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see your card details. Stripe processes your payment details under Stripe's privacy policy.

Children

PULSE is suitable for all ages and the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. In practice, the only personal data we might hold is a mailing-list email address or a website subscription record. Email us and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If we ever start collecting new kinds of data in the app, we'll say so here before the update is released.
