Questions

Is the pattern really the same for everyone?

Yes. Each day's pattern and twist come from the UK date, so everyone playing on the same UK day gets the same sequence. It changes at midnight UK time.

Do I need to download anything?

No. PULSE runs in your browser on your phone or computer. Your streak and best score are saved on the device you play on, so clearing your browser data or switching device starts them again. PULSE+ can be unlocked on another device with the link from the PULSE+ box.

How long does a game take?

Most runs last one to three minutes. Strong players who clear many rounds will take longer.

What does PULSE+ include?

You can play any of the last 30 days, practise with unlimited random patterns and change the pad colours. The daily game stays free.

How do I use PULSE+ on another phone or computer?

Open the PULSE+ box on the device where you subscribed and press Copy link for another device. Open that link on the other device and PULSE+ unlocks there too. Keep the link private, because anyone who has it can unlock PULSE+.

How do I cancel PULSE+?

Use the Manage subscription button in the PULSE+ box. You keep PULSE+ until the end of the period you have paid for.

Will there be a phone app?

Apps for iOS and Android are planned. Join the list and we'll tell you when they launch, and how PULSE+ will work in them.

Can I replay today's pattern?

Yes. Try again as often as you like. Your best score for the day is the one that counts.
