A new twist every midnight

The daily pattern is the same for every player in the UK, but the rules change. Seven twists rotate, so no two days feel the same.

Classic

Repeat the pattern exactly as it flashed.

Mirror

Tap the mirror image, with left and right swapped.

Reverse

Play the pattern backwards, last pad first.

Ghost

Flashes are lightning quick. Blink and you miss it.

Surge

Everything runs 40% faster. Hold your nerve.

Rotate

Tap the pattern turned a quarter turn clockwise.

Blackout

Every pad turns the same grey. Remember positions, not colours.

It gets harder the longer you last

Every run climbs through four stages. Each one keeps everything from the stage before.

1 · Warm-up

Rounds 1 to 3. Just today's twist.

2 · Decoys

From round 4, hollow fake flashes sneak into the pattern. Ignore them.

3 · Double twist

From round 7, a second twist stacks on the first. Mirror and Reverse at once, anyone?

4 · Countdown

From round 10, every tap is on a timer that keeps getting shorter.

Built to be shared, not scrolled

Three lives, one go

Miss a pad and you lose a life. Clear every round you can before the third miss.

Result card

Your run becomes a grid of green and red squares you can paste into any group chat without spoiling the pattern.

Streaks

Come back each day to grow your streak. Compare scores with your friends and see who lasts longest.

Questions

Is the pattern really the same for everyone?

Yes. Each day's pattern and twist come from the UK date, so everyone playing on the same UK day gets the same sequence. It changes at midnight UK time.

Do I need to download anything?

No. PULSE runs in your browser on your phone or computer. Your streak and best score are saved on the device you play on, so clearing your browser data or switching device starts them again. PULSE+ can be unlocked on another device with the link from the PULSE+ box.

How long does a game take?

Most runs last one to three minutes. Strong players who clear many rounds will take longer.

What does PULSE+ include?

You can play every past daily, practise any twist starting from any stage, change the pad colours, and you get one free Streak Saver every week. The daily game stays free.

Which PULSE+ option should I pick?

Yearly is the best value at £11.99, about £1 a month, and starts with a 7-day free trial. Monthly is £1.99 if you'd rather try it for a while. Forever is £19.99 once, for anyone who doesn't want a subscription at all.

How do Streak Savers work?

If you miss a day, a Streak Saver is used the next time you open PULSE so your streak carries on. One saver covers one missed day. You can buy 3 for £0.99, and PULSE+ members get one free each week. Savers are kept on the device you play on.

How do I use PULSE+ on another phone or computer?

Open the PULSE+ box on the device where you bought PULSE+ and press Copy link for another device. Open that link on the other device and PULSE+ unlocks there too. Keep the link private, because anyone who has it can unlock PULSE+.

How do I cancel PULSE+?

Use the Manage subscription button in the PULSE+ box. You keep PULSE+ until the end of the period you have paid for. PULSE+ Forever is a single payment, so there is nothing to cancel.

Will there be a phone app?

Apps for iOS and Android are planned. Join the list and we'll tell you when they launch, and how PULSE+ will work in them.

Can I replay today's pattern?

Yes. Try again as often as you like. Your best score for the day is the one that counts.
